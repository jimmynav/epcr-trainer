How this works
  1. You arrive on scene with the CAD times shown in the dispatch panel. Size up the scene before making patient contact.
  2. Assess, question, treat, and reassess using the action buttons. Findings and patient responses appear in the call log with the time they happened.
  3. Chart as you go or after the call. Times in your chart should match the CAD and the call log.
  4. When the call is over, finish your chart, run the validation check, and generate your PCR. Save it as a PDF and upload it where your instructor tells you.
